How we work

A four-step engagement, run the same way every time.

No endless discovery, no consultant theatre. We move from first call to first deliverable inside a defined, accountable process.

01

Scoping conversation

A 45-minute call with the partner who'll actually lead your engagement. We map the mandate, the constraints and the decision timeline — before any fee discussion.

02

Diagnostic & proposal

We review documentation, models and existing frameworks, then send a written proposal with a defined scope, deliverables, timeline and fixed fee. No surprises later.

03

Delivery & review

Work is delivered in stages, with a partner-led review at every gate. Nothing leaves our office without two pairs of senior eyes on it.

04

Ongoing stewardship

Most mandates continue into monitoring, quarterly reviews or retained advisory. We're here for the multi-year arc, not the single transaction.

Common questions

What institutions ask us first.

If your question isn't here, our team responds to substantive enquiries within one business day.

Our clients include tier-1 and regional banks, asset managers, corporate treasuries, family offices, insurers and public sector institutions. Most engagements sit with institutions managing at least $1 billion in assets or exposure, though we do selectively work with earlier-stage managers where the mandate fits.

Most engagements are delivered on a fixed-fee basis, scoped and agreed in writing before work begins. Retained advisory is available on a monthly or quarterly basis for ongoing mandates. We do not bill hourly — it creates the wrong incentives on both sides.

Every mandate is led by a partner from first conversation to final deliverable. Junior team members support execution, but never replace the partner's involvement in client decisions. This is a structural commitment, not a marketing line.

Yes. We're happy to execute a mutual NDA before the first substantive conversation. Our standard form is available on request, or we'll work with your template.

We maintain a strict conflicts register and clear each new mandate against it before engaging. Where a potential conflict exists, we disclose it in writing and either decline the mandate or put in place information barriers agreed with the client.

Diagnostic work typically runs 3–6 weeks. Full mandates — allocation frameworks, regulatory remediations, capital transactions — usually span 3–9 months. Retained advisory is open-ended and reviewed quarterly.
