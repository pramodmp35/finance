About STACKLY

Built by practitioners, for institutions that measure trust in decades.

STACKLY was founded in 2009 by a small group of capital markets, risk and treasury professionals who were tired of watching good institutions receive mediocre advice. We set out to build a firm where every mandate is led by a partner, every model is documented, and every deliverable is written to survive review.

  • Partner-led on every engagement — no junior handoffs, no silent scope creep.
  • Independent by structure — no proprietary products, no placement fees, no conflicts register exceptions.
  • Regulator-grade documentation on every model, framework and recommendation we ship.

Common questions

What institutions ask us first.

If your question isn't here, our team responds to substantive enquiries within one business day.

Our clients include tier-1 and regional banks, asset managers, corporate treasuries, family offices, insurers and public sector institutions. Most engagements sit with institutions managing at least $1 billion in assets or exposure, though we do selectively work with earlier-stage managers where the mandate fits.

Scoping calls are typically scheduled within 48 hours. For mandates that don't require conflicts clearance, we can usually begin diagnostic work inside two weeks of a signed proposal. Urgent regulatory matters are triaged the same day.

Every mandate is led by a partner from first conversation to final deliverable. Junior team members support execution, but never replace the partner's involvement in client decisions. This is a structural commitment, not a marketing line.

Yes. We're happy to execute a mutual NDA before the first substantive conversation. Our standard form is available on request, or we'll work with your template.

We maintain a strict conflicts register and clear each new mandate against it before engaging. Where a potential conflict exists, we disclose it in writing and either decline the mandate or put in place information barriers agreed with the client.

For retained advisory, our Essentials tier starts at $2,400 per month. For project mandates, we typically scope work with a minimum fee of $40,000. We occasionally take on smaller engagements where the mandate is strategically interesting or the institution is a first-time client.
