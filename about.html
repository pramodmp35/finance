Our Story

Fifteen years of doing the unglamorous work.

We didn't start with a pitch deck. We started with a problem the industry had spent a decade avoiding — and a belief that financial infrastructure should be treated as seriously as the money it moves.

In 2009, three former risk officers and a settlement engineer sat in a rented office in Canary Wharf and asked a question that most of the industry was too bruised to entertain: what would it take to rebuild financial plumbing from first principles?

The answer was unglamorous. It meant years of regulated conversations, careful ledger design, and refusing to ship features that solved the wrong problems. It meant building governance before growth, and audibility before ambition. It meant understanding that trust is earned in decades and lost in a single settlement.

Fifteen years later, that discipline is still the operating system of everything we do. We serve banks, asset managers, corporate treasuries and family offices across 47 markets. We've settled over $4.2 trillion in payment volume without a single material incident.

Vision & Mission

Why we exist, and what we're building toward.

Two statements we return to when decisions get hard — and when they get easy.

Our Vision

A financial system where every institution — regardless of size or geography — operates on infrastructure that is transparent, auditable and resilient by default. Where the plumbing beneath capital markets is no longer an afterthought, but the first design decision.

Our Mission

To serve the institutions that move capital with the discipline, governance and reliability that serious money requires. We build infrastructure that regulators can inspect, auditors can verify, and treasurers can depend on — without compromise, and without shortcuts.
